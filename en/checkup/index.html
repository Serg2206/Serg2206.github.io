Frequently asked questions

Questions and Answers

Do I need to book a medical checkup in advance?
Yes. MARIA Medical Center works by appointment so you can be seen at the scheduled time without queues.
Which documents are required for a medical checkup?
A passport or another identity document. Corporate examinations may also require an employer referral stating the occupation and workplace factors.
How long does a medical checkup take?
Depending on the type of examination, from 30 minutes to 2–3 hours. A basic checkup usually takes about one hour; a comprehensive checkup may require two visits.
Can an entire team receive corporate checkups?
Yes. MARIA Medical Center works with companies and organizations. Terms and schedules are arranged in advance.
Which documents are needed for a driving medical certificate?
A passport and a 3×4 photograph are required. Confirm the current examination list and certificate validity when booking.
How long is a medical certificate valid?
Validity depends on the certificate type, category, and current regulations. Confirm it when the certificate is issued.
Can I have a checkup on the day I come in?
Yes, a basic checkup and certificate take one visit (about an hour). If additional tests are needed, the certificate is issued the next day. A comprehensive Check-up may require 2 visits.
What does the comprehensive Check-up include?
An individual program: consultation with an internist and a surgeon, complete blood count and urinalysis, blood chemistry, ECG, abdominal ultrasound, chest X-ray. Depending on age and risks: mammography, Pap test, tumor markers, colonoscopy.
Do I need to fast before a checkup?
Yes, if blood will be taken for chemistry and glucose — do not eat for 8–12 hours (water is allowed). Abdominal ultrasound is also done on an empty stomach. If the checkup is only for a certificate without tests, fasting is not needed. Please ask when booking.
Do you issue certificates for work abroad?
Yes. We issue medical certificates (form 082/o) for employment, study and sports events abroad. The certificate is issued in English in the international format, stamped by the center.
