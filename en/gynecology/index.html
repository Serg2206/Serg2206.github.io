When to see a doctor

Symptoms Requiring Consultation

Do not ignore these symptoms — early diagnosis makes treatment easier and improves the prognosis.

🔴
Menstrual irregularities

Irregular, painful, heavy or scanty periods are a reason to see a gynecologist.

🔴
Lower abdominal pain

Chronic or acute pain may indicate inflammation, endometriosis, a cyst or other conditions.

🟡
Abnormal discharge

A change in the character, color or smell of discharge requires ruling out infection and other diseases.

🟡
Difficulty conceiving

No pregnancy after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse is an indication for investigation.

🟡
Annual checkup

Even without complaints, an annual preventive checkup can detect conditions at an early stage.

Frequently asked questions

Questions and Answers

How should I prepare for a gynecology appointment?
If the situation is not urgent, confirm the most suitable examination time when booking. Bring previous ultrasound reports, test results, and a medication list.
How often should I see a gynecologist?
The frequency of preventive visits depends on age, medical history, and risk factors. A physician should recommend an individual schedule.
Does Professor Sushkov see patients without a referral?
Yes. A referral is not required; book by phone or through an available messenger.
Can gynecologic surgery be performed laparoscopically?
Many gynecologic operations can be performed laparoscopically. Suitability depends on the diagnosis, extent of surgery, and the patient’s condition.
Can the uterus be preserved when treating fibroids?
In many cases, myomectomy can remove fibroids while preserving the uterus. The decision depends on fibroid size, number and location, symptoms, and reproductive plans.
Which symptoms require urgent medical attention?
Seek urgent medical care for severe pain, heavy bleeding, fainting, high fever, or sudden deterioration.
What is colposcopy and when is it needed?
Colposcopy examines the cervix under magnification. It may be recommended after an abnormal Pap test, HPV detection, or identification of a suspicious cervical area.
Can I become pregnant after gynecologic laparoscopy?
Pregnancy is possible in many cases. Timing depends on the diagnosis, extent of surgery, and recovery and should be discussed with the treating physician.
Which tests are needed before gynecologic surgery?
Requirements are individualized and commonly include blood tests, coagulation assessment, ECG, and imaging; additional studies are ordered when indicated.
How long is recovery after gynecologic surgery?
Recovery depends on the type and extent of surgery, surgical approach, and the patient’s condition. The surgeon provides individual restrictions.
What is endometriosis and how is it treated?
Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the uterine lining is found outside the uterine cavity. Treatment is individualized.
