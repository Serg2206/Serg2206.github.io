Biography

Surgeon, scientist, teacher

Sergiy Valentinovich Sushkov is a Doctor of Medical Sciences, Professor and surgical oncologist with more than 40 years of continuous clinical and research work. He is one of the pioneers of laparoscopic surgery in the Kharkiv region: he performed his first video-endoscopic operations back in 1995–1997, long before such equipment became widespread in Ukraine.

For 22 years he served as Deputy Director for Research at the V.T. Zaitsev Institute of General and Emergency Surgery of the National Academy of Medical Sciences of Ukraine — one of the country's leading surgical centers. Under his leadership, dozens of clinical studies were carried out and many PhD and doctoral theses were defended.

Today Sergiy Valentinovich runs a surgical and consulting practice at MARIA Medical Center (Kharkiv), treating patients with the most complex diagnoses in abdominal surgery, surgical oncology and gynecology.

Specialty

Areas of expertise

Professor Sushkov specializes in the most complex cases, which require a combination of surgical skill, oncological vigilance and in-depth knowledge of anatomy.

01
Laparoscopic surgery
Karl Storz Full HD. Cholecystectomy, hernias, kidney cysts, biopsy, lymph node dissection. Recovery in 2–4 days.
02
Surgical Oncology
Surgical treatment of stomach, bowel, breast and female reproductive cancers. Histological verification.
03
Gynecologic oncology
Laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgery. Treatment of cervical, ovarian and endometrial cancer.
04
Myomectomy
Removal of uterine fibroids with full preservation of the uterus. Fibroids of any size and location. Restoration of fertility.
05
Cancer Diagnostics
Cancer screening. Expert-class ultrasound, tumor markers, colposcopy. Results interpreted by a professor.
06
Emergency surgery
Appendicitis, peritonitis, strangulated hernias, bowel obstruction. Experience in managing critically ill patients in emergencies.
